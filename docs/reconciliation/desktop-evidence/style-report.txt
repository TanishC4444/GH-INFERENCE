INT. AIRLOCK - NIGHT

Emergency lighting casts crimson shadows across the cracked viewport. The hiss of escaping atmosphere carries a metallic tang. The oxygen gauge flickers: 12%.

MIRA (40s) frantically adjusts a valve. Her hands shake slightly. SOL (40s) leans against the bulkhead, arms crossed, watching her.

MIRA
The seals won't hold another cycle. The pod's manual override requires someone to stay behind and cycle the pressure release.

SOL
(Dryly)
Then you should go. You're the one who insisted we take the scenic route through the asteroid belt. Your navigation choices are legendary.

MIRA
Don't deflect. The pod only seats one pilot. The other stays to stabilize the systems until the pod detaches.

SOL
I'm not a hero, Mira. I'm just a guy who hates being trapped in small spaces with people who lecture him about safety protocols.

Mira freezes. Her knuckles whiten against the wrench.

MIRA
I'm not lecturing. I'm calculating survival.

SOL
You're calculating logistics. I'm calculating the fact that you're the only one who knows how to calibrate the nav-com.

Mira looks at him. "Logically," she says, "I should stay."

Sol's smirk falters. His gaze softens.

SOL
The pod's manual override is already primed. Just get in.

Mira's grip loosens. "The manual says—"

SOL
Forget the manual. Just move.

INT. FAILING AIRLOCK - NIGHT

*The emergency light flickers. The airlock hisses, venting precious air into space. Two figures stand frozen in the shrinking space between them.*

**Mira:** (checking her gauge) "It's down to twenty percent. We have maybe—" *She stops, looking at Sol with something unreadable in her eyes.*

**Sol:** *Crosses his arms, gaze fixed on the wall.* "Don't say it. I already know. One of us walks out. Other stays here." *His jaw tightens.* "Standard protocol. You're the engineer. You should go."

**Mira:** *Steps closer, voice strained.* "I'm not leaving you here to die, Sol. Not after—" *She catches herself, looking away.* "I can survive longer. My metabolism is better suited for—"

**Sol:** *Turns sharply.* "That's not how this works! You don't get to play hero, not with me." *His hand reaches out, stops short of touching her shoulder.* "Dammit, Mira. We used to be—" *He pauses, swallowing hard.* "...We're not them anymore."

*The airlock groans. They're closer now, forced by the narrowing space.*

**Mira:** *Softly, almost imperceptibly.* "Maybe not. But I can still see you're scared." *A small, sad smile.* "I'm scared too."

MIRA
